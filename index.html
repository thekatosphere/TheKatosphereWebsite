Welcome to theKatosphere.

I'm so glad you're here! I'm Kat Serrano: author, mother, storyteller and co-host of Future Ancestors. I produce podcasts, documentaries and shorts through Audio Vision, and on Sundays I like to write Sunday Kitchen Table, my letters on what I'm living and learning. This is where it all comes together: my perspective on life, and the legacy I'm leaving behind.

What am I passing down?

Every part of my life comes back to this question. The book I wrote, and the ones still to come. The people I sit with on Future Ancestors. The stories I help bring to life behind the camera.

theKatosphere is for the hopeful, the curious and anyone looking to be inspired. Wherever you're from and wherever you're headed, there's a seat for you here. I hope you leave feeling loved, seen and heard.
